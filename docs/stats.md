# Listening stats

**Stats** in the sidebar shows what you've been listening to. It's all worked out from a log kept
on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## What's on the page

- **Totals** along the top: listening time **all time**, the number of **plays**, and time
  **this week**.
- **Per week**: a bar chart of listening time for the last 12 weeks. Weeks start on Monday.
- **Most played**: your top 30 songs by number of plays (ties go to whichever you've listened to
  longer), with each one's play count and total listening time. Click a row to play that song.

The page loads its numbers when you open it, so switch away and back to refresh it.

## How listening is counted

Lumen keeps track of how long you actually listen to each song:

- Only **time spent playing** counts. Paused time doesn't, and neither do the bits you skip by
  seeking forward.
- When the song changes, that listen is saved, as long as it was at least **5 seconds**.
  Anything shorter (skipping through) isn't saved at all.
- A listen counts as a **play** once you've heard **half the song or 4 minutes, whichever comes
  first**. So a 3-minute song counts after 1½ minutes, and a 20-minute song counts after 4.
- Shorter listens still add to **listening time**, just not to the play count.
- Each pass of a song on **repeat one** is counted separately.

The song that's playing when you close the window or quit Lumen is saved too.

## Privacy and clearing history

Under **⚙ Settings → Listening stats**:

- The **switch** turns recording on and off. When it's off, nothing is saved at all. Already-saved
  history stays until you clear it. The switch takes effect straight away, without pressing Save.
- **Clear history** deletes every saved listen. Click it, then click **Tap again to confirm**
  within 3 seconds. This can't be undone.

## Good to know

- If a song is removed from the library (its file was deleted or moved and you rescanned), its
  history goes with it.
- [Starting the library over](settings-and-data.md#starting-over) clears stats too.
