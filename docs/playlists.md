# Playlists

Your playlists are listed in the sidebar under **Playlists**, each with its song count.

## Creating one

Click **＋** next to *Playlists*, type a name and press **Create**. The name box suggests a random
example each time; ignore it or steal it.

## Adding songs

- **One song**: right-click it (or click **⋯**). The bottom of the menu lists your playlists, so
  click one.
- **Several songs**: click **Edit** at the top of any list, tick the songs, then **Add to
  playlist…** in the bar at the bottom.

New songs go on the end. A song can only be in a playlist once, so adding it again does
nothing.

## Playing, removing and ordering

- Click a playlist to open it. **Play all** or click a song to start, just like any other list.
- Remove a song: right-click it inside the playlist → **Remove from playlist**.
- Songs play in the order they were added. There's no way to reorder songs inside a playlist
  yet, and playlists have no sort control.

## Managing playlists

Hover a playlist in the sidebar to see its buttons:

- **✏️ Rename**
- **× Delete**. You're asked to confirm. Only the playlist is deleted, not the songs.

**Drag** playlists up and down the sidebar to reorder them. The order is saved.

## Good to know

- Playlists only live inside Lumen. There's no import or export of `.m3u` files.
- If a song's file is moved or renamed, it drops out of every playlist after the next rescan
  ([why](library.md#moving-and-renaming-files)).
